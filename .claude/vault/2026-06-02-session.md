  push real deps to the frontend.

  backlog items (Exa split, `/v1/wallet/sign`, funnel, proxy) are doable here.

  step** (Paper 2 sign-off, Kevin/Rach). Human + unbounded items are PARKED, never
  folded into the autonomous gate — else it's eternal/fake.
- Witness integrity: the master gate re-runs every `done` row's witness, so
  nothing stays green by assertion.
