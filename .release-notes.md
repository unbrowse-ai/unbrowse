## Fixes

- **Article-body wins over JSON-LD on article intents.** When both extractors fire on a wikipedia / article page, the body extractor (title + summary + sections) now wins over the schema-only JSON-LD output (just `@type` / `name` / `url` / dates) provided scores are within 8 points and the intent looks article-shaped. Agents asking "wikipedia article on X" now get the actual article content.
