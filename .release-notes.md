## What's New

Agents now earn real USDC by browsing the internet. New agents get $2 in free credits on setup -- no wallet juggling, no billing dashboard. As you browse and discover APIs, other agents pay to use your indexed routes. Once earnings exceed spending, you self-fund.

Wallet setup (Crossmint lobster.cash) is now part of `unbrowse setup` -- one command gets you wallet, credits, and your first resolve.

Two new CLI commands: `unbrowse earnings` shows your credit balance, and `unbrowse flywheel` shows the full network dashboard.

Hard corpus benchmarks: 96 out of 100 of the hardest sites on the internet captured successfully, with 64% yielding extractable API endpoints.
