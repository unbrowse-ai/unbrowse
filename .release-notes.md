## Fixes

- **Article extraction now passes the quality gate.** v6.2.2 fired the article extractor on real wikipedia pages but `validateExtractionQuality` rejected the result with `confidence too low (0.50 < 0.5)` because `article` wasn't in `computeConfidence`'s switch (fell through to the 0.3 default + small boost = 0.50). v6.2.3 pins article confidence at 0.9 alongside json-ld and itemlist, so the title + summary + sections payload actually reaches the agent.
