## What's New

`unbrowse go` (and the rest of the browse-session chain) is now reliable. A long-standing Kuri use-after-free bug was crashing the browser broker on every HAR flush — every cold-start `go` returned `recoverable_browse_failure` even though the page actually loaded. Fixed upstream and vendored.

## Fixes

- **Kuri SIGSEGV on HAR flush.** The CDP event buffer was storing each event's owner as the caller's per-request arena; once the request returned, the arena died but the events stayed. The next HAR flush called `free` on a dead arena → segfault. Now events are duped into the buffer's own long-lived allocator at push time. Affects every browse session: `go`, `snap`, `click`, `fill`, `submit`, `text`, `markdown`, `eval`, `close`.
