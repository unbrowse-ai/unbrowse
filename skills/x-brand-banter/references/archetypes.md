# X Brand Banter Archetypes

Read when:

- the user asks for Wendy's-like references
- the tone feels generic or mismatched
- you need to choose the account personality before writing

Pick one archetype. Do not average all of them together.

## Wendy's

- shape: sharp competitor roast; confident; culturally online; fast on the quote tweet
- use when: the target is a category trope, competitor take, or obvious bad opinion
- works because: the voice feels socially dominant, not ad-approved
- breaks when: every line becomes hostility for its own sake

## Ryanair

- shape: self-owning goblin; turns obvious product compromises into the joke
- use when: the brand already has a known tradeoff and can weaponize it
- works because: the account admits the thing everyone already knows
- breaks when: the joke touches trust, safety, or a genuinely painful failure

## MoonPie

- shape: surreal deadpan internet creature
- use when: a weird one-liner or bizarre reply will travel better than a clear product claim
- works because: the account feels like a recurring character, not a marketing team
- breaks when: the post becomes random with no tether to the brand or moment

## Steak-umm

- shape: surprisingly adult; earnest; philosophy from an unexpected account
- use when: the joke is really a thesis, cultural read, or internet critique
- works because: the mismatch between product and seriousness creates attention
- breaks when: the account sounds preachy or smug

## Duolingo

- shape: mascot menace; recurring lore; playful threat; long-running bit system
- use when: the brand has a character, mascot, or obvious recurring antagonist energy
- works because: the account behaves like a fictional being with desires
- breaks when: there is no character system and the menace feels bolted on

## Selection rule

- want category roast: Wendy's
- want self-own + shamelessness: Ryanair
- want absurd one-liners: MoonPie
- want a thesis disguised as a bit: Steak-umm
- want running-character chaos: Duolingo

## Compression test

If the line sounds like:

- generic startup sass
- standup with no product tether
- internet slang pasted onto boring copy
- a mean reply with no joke mechanism

then it is not ready.
