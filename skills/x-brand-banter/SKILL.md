# X Brand Banter

Core job:

- turn bland brand-account copy into one funny, in-character X post, reply, or quote tweet

Use this skill when:

- Lewis wants a brand account to feel like a person worth following
- the ask is for funny X posts, replies, quote tweets, or recurring bits
- the tone should feel like Wendy's, Ryanair, MoonPie, Steak-umm, Duolingo, or similar brand-account archetypes
- the post needs personality, banter, roast energy, deadpan, or mascot menace
- the joke still needs to preserve a real product or category truth

Do not use this skill for:

- generic founder tweeting or personal-brand posting
- long educational threads or explainers
- analytics, campaign diagnosis, or performance reporting
- ad copy, landing-page copy, or email copy
- crisis comms, legal statements, support escalations, or sensitive customer issues

Workflow:

1. Pick one archetype only.
   - roast bully
   - self-own gremlin
   - absurd deadpan weirdo
   - sincere philosopher
   - mascot menace
2. Pick one move only.
   - original post
   - reply
   - quote tweet
   - self-own
   - recurring callback
3. Find one true tension.
   - category hypocrisy
   - product tradeoff
   - user behavior
   - competitor trope
   - internet discourse pattern
4. Aim the punch correctly.
   - punch up at the category, the behavior, the big account, or the brand itself
   - do not punch down at powerless users or sensitive traits
5. Write the line with a brand tether.
   - one joke
   - one truth
   - one product noun, constraint, or known brand trait
6. If it could work unchanged for 20 SaaS accounts, rewrite it.
7. Output 3 to 5 candidates unless the user asks for one final line only.

Load-bearing rules:

- one skill, one job: brand-account banter on X
- one archetype per run; mixed archetypes read as try-hard
- personality is not random meanness
- the funniest line must still be true without the joke
- if the brand is missing from the joke, it is just standup
- keep replies shorter and sharper than original posts
- do not explain the joke
- if trust, safety, legal, or support risk appears, drop the bit and route elsewhere

Default output:

- chosen archetype
- one-sentence angle
- 3 to 5 post/reply candidates
- optional keep / cut note on which one feels most native
