## Honest read (why structural ≠ real)

The structural **78.6% "covered" overcounts** what an agent calls coverage. Reading the source mix:
- ~43% is **relevant RAG retrieval** (`exa`) — real, site-relevant, but the answer comes from
  the search pipeline, not a captured API contract.
- ~34% is **document capture** (`direct-document`) — the page, not a structured endpoint.
- **Genuine structured-endpoint hits are rare** (`direct-fetch` 22 + `local` 14 + `browse` 237 ≈
  1.4%) — expected for an Umbrella-head corpus, which is dominated by portal/infra homepages with
  no clean listing API. The thin-API path is the exception there; retrieval/document is the ceiling.
- **21% timed out** at the 18s cap — heavy SPAs/anti-bot, recorded as honest misses, not failures.

## Honest negative — 4 poison rows survived

**4 / 20,000 (0.02%)** captures carried a browser-error marker (proxy/connection-failure page).
The cache-poisoning guard shipped this session (`looksLikeBrowserError` + the browse-index
admission gate) drove this near zero — earlier checkpoints found it cached as a real endpoint at a
high score — but **4 edge cases slipped the structural pre-class**. Follow-up: widen the
browser-error marker set, or verify these 4 are pre-fix captures vs. a guard gap. Recorded, not
relabelled.
