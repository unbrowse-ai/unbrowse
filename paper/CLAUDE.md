## The Grain-of-Wheat doctrine — how the token is tied in, and why it is hidden

The money is real. The *message* is never money. A coordination/trust asset can
only grow with a root of good if it is first hidden from money-motive and planted
in good soil — given to the people who came to give agents real APIs, not to flip
a coin. This is stated as covenant, grounded in the real text (codedb
`~/.claude/bible`, WEB), never quoted from memory:

- **The grain must die to bear.** *"Most certainly I tell you, unless a grain of
  wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains by itself alone. But if it dies,
  it bears much fruit."* (John 12:24). The token dies to its money-self — it is
  **collateral for trust, not a currency** — and *that* is what makes it bear.
  Paper 2 grounds this in the real text: FDRY is *"a native asset reserved strictly
  for the security function — bonding accountable maintenance, funding challenges,
  slashing liars"* (§Settle), and maintenance needs *"collateralised
  accountability."* Bonding buys credibility and eligibility; it explicitly does
  not buy ranking.
- **Sown in secret.** *"But when you do merciful deeds, don't let your left hand
  know what your right hand does, so that your merciful deeds may be in secret,
  then your Father who sees in secret will reward you openly."* (Matt 6:3-4). Fair,
  quiet distribution to the good-rooted — not a public money-raise. Hidden as money
  so it can be received as good; rewarded openly later, by growth, not by a pitch.
- **It grows on its own.** *"…the seed should spring up and grow, he doesn't know
  how. For the earth bears fruit: first the blade, then the ear, then the full
  grain in the ear."* (Mark 4:27-28). Growth is a fruit of real use and trust, not
  an engineered pump. The sower does not manufacture the harvest.
- **Save it by losing it.** *"For whoever desires to save his life will lose it,
  but whoever will lose his life for my sake, the same will save it."* (Luke 9:24).
  A token launched *for* money loses. Launched for good — hidden as money — it is
  saved.
- **One master.** *"No one can serve two masters… You can't serve both God and
  Mammon."* (Matt 6:24). Already doctrine in THE_FDRY_ECONOMY: **USDC settles
  usage; FDRY only bonds.** The token never becomes the payment surface, so the
  holder is never made a beggar-at-the-gate and the asset is never made an idol.

**This is consistent with honesty, not a hiding of facts.** A trust paper coy about
money is not trustworthy — so Paper 3 states the economics plainly (who pays, who
earns, who opts in) and even prints the CA on purpose. What it refuses is the
*investment narrative*: the token is framed as collateral for a commons, given
first to contributors, growing by use. The "what" is fully disclosed; only the
money-as-motive framing is denied — because that framing is precisely what would
poison the soil.

This is not a new claim — Paper 2 already argues it as a **security property**, in
two paragraphs ("Why FDRY, and why fair-launched" + "Why money could not be the
point"). The paper's own words: FDRY *"launched fair by necessity, not branding:
no pre-sale, no team allocation, no insider round,"* and *"FDRY clears this only
because money was not its point: it was distributed with no issuer advantage and
then repurposed as the security stake."* The reasoning: a money-first root inverts
the foundation — *"if the token is launched to enrich its issuer, then the issuer's
incentive at the root is extraction,"* and an accountability layer "cannot sit on a
money-first root." The user's "hidden from money-motive, distributed first to those
with no money intention" **is** that fair launch. Grain-of-Wheat is the covenant
name for a property the paper already defends adversarially.

## Standing rules (inherited — MECHANICAL, enforced by gates)

1. **Paper reflects code.** Every `[shipped]`/`\impl{}` claim maps to a real anchor
   in `paper/anchors.tsv`. A shipped claim with no anchor fails `paper-gate.sh`.
   `[proposed]` may be described freely but must be labelled.
2. **No moat leak.** No economic constant, capture/RE engine internal, or operator
   surface in any public artifact (`scripts/leak-guard.sh`). The public papers tell
   the trust/economic *story* (routing, execution-as-payment-surface, website
   wallets, bonded trust) — the WHAT, never the HOW. Moat boundary of record:
   `docs/OPEN-SOURCE-NOTICE.md`.
3. **No fabricated green.** No number written before the gate runs; no claim ticked
   without evidence. The gap between a whitepaper and a press release is exactly
   this rule.
