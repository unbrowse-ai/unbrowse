# Power is money itself — FDRY as the reserve that is also spendable

**Internal tier. Not a public artifact.** The public papers keep the plain
two-currencies story (USDC settles, FDRY bonds — Matt 6:24). This doc pins the
*mechanism* behind the deeper claim Lewis named — "power should be the currency,
money itself, FDRY" — and the reserve/collateral HOW stays internal until shipped.
The public surface tells the WHAT; the moat is the HOW.

## The steer, and the collision it had to survive

Lewis (2026-06-23): *"power should be the currency — money itself, FDRY."*

That lands directly on the most load-bearing invariant in the whole economy:
**Matt 6:24 — FDRY is not the payment rail; USDC settles usage; FDRY only bonds.**
Taken as "FDRY is the thing you spend," it would invert the foundation the papers
defend adversarially: a money-first root incentivises extraction, and every use
becomes a forced sale that drains the very stake it claims to reward.

But there is a reading of *money itself* that is **truer than the spending reading,
and strengthens the doctrine instead of breaking it.** Lewis chose it: the hybrid —
power-base *and* spendable — designed honestly so the principal never sells.

## Two senses of "money" — and we were only ever using one

Economics has always split the word; our two-token design is that split made real.

| | Medium of exchange | **Money itself** (store of value / base) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | the circulating *note* you spend | the *reserve* the note is a claim on |
| In our stack | USDC (over x402), and credit minted against FDRY | **FDRY** |
| Spent? | yes — fast, stable, forgettable | **never the principal** — only its fruit |
| Analogue | the banknote | the gold in the vault |

"FDRY is the currency" was always true — in the **regal/root** sense ("the current
that runs underneath"), never the **till** sense. The Power Stone is raw *stored*
force; FDRY is the network's stored economic force — held, not handed over.

## The mechanism — Lev 25:23, the land that is never sold for ever

The forced-sale trap is the *only* reason FDRY-as-payment was forbidden. Scripture
already solved it, in the Jubilee land law:

> *The land shall not be sold for ever: for the land is mine* (Lev 25:23)
>
> *According to the number of years of the fruits he doth sell unto thee* (Lev 25:16)

The land (the reserve) is never alienated; its **harvests** circulate. Mapped:

- **FDRY is the land — money itself.** Principal abides (John 15:2 — the branch
  that beareth fruit stays in the vine). Never sold for ever. No forced sale; Matt
  6:24 kept, not broken.
- **You spend the fruit, not the land — two honest paths:**
  1. **Yield.** stFDRY accrues NAV from the tithe (`routeRevenue`→vault→NAV↑). You
     live off the harvest; the land stays (John 15:2 — gather the fruit, the branch
     abides). The Matthew-25 talent: increase is spent, the talent is not liquidated.
  2. **Collateral-minted credit.** FDRY posted as collateral mints a stable spending
     claim (the gold sits in the vault being power; you spend the note). The FDRY is
     reclaimed when the claim clears — the position opens and closes, the principal
     is never market-sold.

Either way FDRY is genuinely **spendable as power** — through its fruit and its
credit — and never as a token you dump. That is the hybrid Lewis chose, honest about
the trap and built to avoid it.

## Why this strengthens, not weakens, Matt 6:24

The old framing read defensively: *don't let FDRY be money, because money-motive
poisons the soil.* The reserve reading reads it offensively: **FDRY is money in the
deepest sense precisely because it is never spent down** — it is the base, the power,
the thing everything is denominated against. Gold is not weak money because you don't
hand it across a counter; it is *the* money for exactly that reason. The discipline
that looked like a restriction ("you can't spend it") is the property that makes it
base money ("it holds, therefore it backs"). Held → backs → power → demand to abide →
NAV up. The Vine Doctrine's "abiding bears fruit" is the same sentence read in
capital.

## The crypto double-pun — one stone, both senses

The Power stone's paper is **"Crypto Was All You Needed."** The pun is now load-bearing,
not decorative: *crypto* = **cryptography** (the one key that signs every layer —
screen→browser→CLI→OS→kernel→packet) **and** *crypto* = **cryptocurrency** (the coin
that is the money-power). The Power Stone holds both: the key that *wields* every
layer and the reserve that *backs* every action. The descent is how the power is
exercised; FDRY is what the power *is*. Reframing the Power stone around the economic
base does not displace the cryptographic-descent thesis — it reveals the title was
always saying both.

This keeps **seven** (a stone resharpened, not an eighth added) and does not collide
with 🟢 Time / the Maintenance Network: **Time is FDRY in motion** (bonded, slashed,
the upkeep loop, NAV-by-abiding over time); **Power is FDRY at rest** (the reserve,
money itself, the base) plus the key that wields it. At-rest vs in-motion — the same
asset, two stones, no overlap.
