# FDRY Token Disclosure

**$FDRY is the Unbrowse token.** This page states that plainly and publishes the
verifiable on-chain details so the asset is accurately and unambiguously described.

## $FDRY is the Unbrowse token

$FDRY is the native token of Unbrowse. It is the coordination asset for the
Unbrowse Maintenance Network: economically accountable participation in
higher-trust route maintenance, bonding, and dispute resolution. There is exactly
one official $FDRY, and these are its on-chain details.

## You do not need FDRY to use Unbrowse

Ordinary route access is settled in stable-denominated terms. A user never has to
hold FDRY, price anything in token units, or think about it to resolve and execute
routes. The token's role is the maintenance and accountability layer, not everyday
usage.
